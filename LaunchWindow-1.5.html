New Features

  • Instant list updates: Automatically detects when apps are installed or uninstalled without needing a manual refresh everytime.
  • Layout Backup (Export & Import): Back up your entire app order, folders, and preferences to a .json file and restore them anytime on any device.
  • Persistent Auto-Sort A-Z Toggle: A-Z sorting is now a toggle instead of a one time button.
  • Auto-Clear Search: Automatically clears the search bar after dismissing the grid

Visuals & Animations

  • Added Gaussian blur-fade animations when opening/closing the grid, searching etc, feels MUCH more polished now.
  • Micro animations: Added subtle hover, pressing, springy animations.
  • Updated the panel finish with a subtle dark tint.

Performance & Bug Fixes

  • Instant Grid closing: grid now instantly closes when an app is launched instead of waiting for it.
  • Scrolling & Search: fixed scrolling, and heavy blur lag, significantly improving the performance.
